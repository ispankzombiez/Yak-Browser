YakBrowser

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the YakBrowser Android application ("the App") handles user information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a safe experience.

Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or share any personal data from users. This includes, but is not limited to:

  • No personal information
  • No device information
  • No usage data
  • No analytics
  • No cookies or tracking technologies
  • No third-party data collection

Settings Stored on Your Device

The App keeps a small set of preferences — such as the orientation-lock and keep-screen-on switches, a session counter used only to decide when to show the occasional rating prompt, and the last version you updated from — in local storage on your device. These settings never leave your phone: they are not transmitted to us, to Yakkamon, or to any other third party, and uninstalling the App removes them.

Permissions

The App requests only the permissions required for its core functionality (such as internet access to display the game). No data accessed through permissions is collected, transmitted, or stored by the App.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or analytics tools that collect user data.

  • The App displays the external website yakkamon.com, which is operated by a third party and is subject to its own privacy policy. This App has no control over, and receives nothing from, that site.
  • The App may occasionally show Google Play's built-in rating prompt (In-App Review). It contains no analytics and sends nothing to the App's developers; ratings are handled entirely by Google Play.

Children's Privacy

Because the App does not collect any data, it is safe for all users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If we make any changes, the updated Privacy Policy will be posted on this page. You are encouraged to review this page periodically for updates.
